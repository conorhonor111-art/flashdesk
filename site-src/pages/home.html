What to do

Four steps, read over the phone.

  1. 1Download the file and open it. Nothing installs.
  2. 2It shows you a 9-digit number — like a phone number for this computer. It is not a password.
  3. 3Read that number to the person helping you.
  4. 4Ask them to read you their number too, and check it matches the one on your screen when they connect. If it does not match, press Reject.
What no competitor can say

The first time anyone connects, a human has to press Accept. There is no way around it, and no way to do it for them.

You watch everything

Your helper sees and controls your screen — while you watch everything they do.

Accept, the first time

The first connection always shows an Accept / Reject dialog — nothing gets in until you press it. If your connection briefly drops, reconnecting within 90 seconds doesn't ask again; that is the same session resuming, not a new one.

Can't be hidden

A band across the top of the window turns amber while anyone is connected. They cannot turn it off.

End it any time

One click, at any moment, ends the session immediately.

Nothing left behind

It does not run when you close it. Close the window and it is gone — nothing was installed.
